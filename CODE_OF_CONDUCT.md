# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Zadaci to be a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful, including toward people you disagree with
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Owning our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing someone's private information without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **thecodingmontana@gmail.com**. Every report is reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code and issues that break this code, and may warn, suspend or ban anyone involved, depending on how serious it is.
